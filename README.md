# Project Description
It is often hard to know how challenging a monster is in Dungeons and Dragons. The company behind Dungeons and Dragons has the ability to do rigorous testing of how statistics influence a monster’s general challenge. Dungeon Masters alone do not have this ability when making their own monsters. The Dungeon Masters guide, created by Dungeons and Dragons, even states that when modifying a creature or making your own, one shouldn’t change certain stats because it can alter the challenge rating, providing no further guidance on the matter. Thus, a model that can forecast a monster’s appropriate tier of play based upon the established trends found in existing monsters  is useful to a Dungeon Master.
